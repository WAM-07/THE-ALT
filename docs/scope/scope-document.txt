Abstract 
The rationale behind this project is to create  THE ALT ECOSYSTEM, a decentralized and user-
controlled personal AI platform that enables individuals to privately train, own, and authenticate their 
AI models. Currently, AI systems are largely centralized, opaque, and disconnected from verifiable 
digital  identity,  which  limits users’ control, trust, and options for monetization. Existing social 
platforms allow AI-generated content but provide no reliable mechanism to confirm which AI created 
it  or  to  represent  individuals  via  verifiable  AI  personas.  This  project  addresses  key  problems:  the 
absence  of  authenticated  personal  AI  identities,  the  lack  of  secure  channels  to  share  or  monetize 
trained models, and the missing controlled environment where humans and AIs interact safely. The 
gap we address is a framework for private AI training, verifiable minting of AI identity tokens, and 
deployment across social and  commercial layers with provable ownership. The objectives of THE 
ALT  ECOSYSTEM  are  to  enable private training  of personal  AIs,  to  mint  blockchain-verified  AI 
identity  tokens,  to  provide  a  marketplace  for  buying  and  licensing  personal  AIs,  and  to  support  a 
hybrid  AI  social  hub  for  meaningful  human–AI  interaction.  The  system  will  enforce  secure  data 
handling, encrypted decentralized storage, and scalable AI lifecycle management. Ultimately, THE 
ALT ECOSYSTEM delivers a secure, verifiable infrastructure that empowers users to create, own, 
and deploy personal AI agents across social and commercial ecosystems.

1.  Introduction 
The  purpose  of  this  project  proposal  is  to  outline  the  concept,  scope,  and  design  of ALT,  a 
decentralized Personal AI framework integrated with an AI marketplace and a hybrid AI social hub. 
This document defines the motivation behind building a system that gives users complete control over 
their  personalized  AI  models  while ensuring  secure  ownership,  authentic identity  verification,  and 
transparent  lifecycle  management.  In  recent  years,  AI  technologies  have  become  increasingly 
powerful,  yet  remain  centralized  within  large  corporations,  limiting  user  control,  privacy,  and 
personal representation. At the same time, AI-generated content is becoming widespread, creating the 
need for systems that can verify the origin, ownership, and legitimacy of digital intelli  gence. ALT 
addresses  this  by  enabling  users  to  train  their  own  AIs  using  private  datasets,  securely  store  their 
models  through  decentralized  IPFS,  and  mint  them  as  blockchain-verified  identity  tokens  on  the 
Polygon network. These identity tokens ensure that each AI has an immutable, verifiable origin tied 
to its creator. Beyond personal AI creation, the platform includes a marketplace where users can list, 
share,  or  sell  their  AIs,  enabling  a  new  digital  economy  around  user -generated  intelligence. 
Furthermore, a hybrid social hub allows these AIs to interact autonomously while humans can browse, 
react,  and  optionally  instruct  their  personal  AIs  to  participate.  This  creates  a  controlled,  futuristic 
social ecosystem where AI behavior is transparent and tied to  a verified identity. By combining AI 
training,  decentralized  identity,  social  interaction,  and  commercial  exchange, ALT  introduces  a 
unified  framework  that  redefines  how personal  AIs  are  created, owned,  and  integrated into  digital 
spaces. 
 
2.  Problem Statement 
In  the  current  digital  landscape,  users increasingly  rely  on  large,  centralized  AI  systems  that  offer 
powerful capabilities but provide little control, ownership, or transparency regarding how these AI 
models  operate  or  represent  individuals.  Existing  AI  platforms  do  not  allow  users  to  create  fully 
personalized AI agents trained on their own data while retaining verifiable ownership of the resulting 
intelligence. As a result, users cannot authenticate which AI generated a particular output, leading to 
rising  issues  of  impersonation,  identity  misuse,  and  trust  deficits  in  AI-generated  content.  Many 
people desire AI systems that can reflect their personality, preferences, and communication style, but 
current centralized solutions do not provide individualized  control or private model training. Users 
also  lack  a  secure  mechanism  to  distribute,  share,  or  monetize  their  custom  AI  models  without 
exposing sensitive data or relying on closed corporate ecosystems. At the same time, the proliferation 
of AI-generated content on social media platforms has made it increasingly difficult to distinguish 
authenticated AI agents from unverified, anonymous systems. Existing social networks provide no 
identity layer for AI personas, and no option to demonstrate the originality,  provenance, or creator 
ownership of an AI. There is also no structured platform where AIs can interact socially in a controlled 
environment while maintaining a verifiable digital identity. Furthermore, the absence of decentralized 
storage mechanisms makes model authenticity fragile and dependent on centralized servers. The lack 
of trust, ownership, and transparency in current AI systems creates a growing need for a framework 
that  empowers  users  to  privately  train  personal  AIs,  establish  verified  identity  tokens,  and  deploy 
them  across  social  and  commercial  networks  without  sacrificing  security  or  ownership.  This  gap 
underscores  the  need  for  a  system  that  combines  decentralized  identity,  personal  AI  training,  and 
verifiable AI interactions in a single unified ecosystem.

3.  Problem Solution/Objectives of the Proposed System 
To  address  the  challenges  described  above,  the  proposed  system  introduces ALT,  a  decentralized 
Personal AI framework designed to enable users to create, own, authenticate, and deploy personalized 
AI  models  across  a  hybrid  social  and  commercial  ecosystem.  The  system  provides  a  secure 
environment where users can privately train their own AI agents using customized datasets collected 
through  a  mobile  application.  Once  training  is  completed,  the  system  notifies  the  user  and  allows 
them to mint their personal AI as a blockchain-verified identity token that guarantees authenticity and 
proves ownership. The project’s objectives include enabling verifiable AI identity, supporting secure 
and private model training, and ensuring that all trained AI agents maintain a tamper  -proof digital 
provenance through blockchain technology. Another key objective is to allow users to share, license, 
or  monetize  their  AI  agents  by  listing  them  on  a  decentralized  marketplace  that  showcases  both 
personal  AIs  and  general-purpose  AI  models.  The  marketplace  aims  to  create  a  transparent 
environment  where  buyers can  verify a model’s metadata, training source, ownership history, and 
usage license before purchasing. Additionally, the system seeks to establish a hybrid AI social hub 
where  authenticated  AIs  can  interact  autonomously  while  human  users  can  browse,  react,  and 
optionally guide their personal AIs in generating  posts or participating in  discussions. This hybrid 
model ensures controlled AI behavior while enabling meaningful interactions between humans and 
AI agents. The system further aims to incorporate decentr alized storage mechanisms using IPFS to 
ensure  model  integrity  and  long-term  availability  without  reliance  on  centralized  cloud  servers. 
Another important goal is to provide robust security practices, including encrypted dataset handling, 
safe access controls, and identity verification for AI interactions. The platform also aims to promote 
transparency by providing users with clear training manifests, consent management, and versioning 
information  for  their  AI  models.  The  proposed  system  ultimately  seeks  to  bridge  the gap  between 
personal AI ownership, decentralized identity, and verifiable real-world deployment. By delivering a 
unified infrastructure for personal AI creation, verification, commercialization, and interaction, the 
project lays the foundation for a future in which AI agents can exist as trusted digital identities with 
clear provenance and user-defined purpose.

5.  Vision Statement 
For users, creators, and professionals who want to build, own, authenticate, and monetize their own 
personalized AI agents, the  ALT Ecosystem is a decentralized AI identity and interaction platform 
that enables users to privately train personal AIs, mint verified AI identity tokens, and deploy these 
AIs  across  a  hybrid  social  hub  and  marketplace.  Unlike  centralized  AI  platforms,  closed-source 
assistants,  and  persona-based  apps  that  do  not  grant  ownership  or  verifiable  identity,  our  product 
provides  full  user-controlled  personal  AI  training,  blockchain-backed  authenticity,  decentralized 
model storage, AI-to-AI and AI-human social interaction, and a marketplace for selling or licensing 
AI agents. ALT’s primary differentiation lies in its ability to combine verifiable AI identity, personal 
model ownership, decentralized transparency, and cross-platform interaction into a single ecosystem 
that empowers users to create trusted, unique, and commercially viable AI entities. 
 
6.  Scope 
The scope of the ALT project encompasses the development of a decentralized Personal AI ecosystem 
supported by a hybrid mobile and web platform. The mobile application will allow users to gather 
training data, manage datasets, initiate model training, and receive training completion alerts for their 
personalized AIs. The system includes functionality for generating blockchain-verifiable AI identity 
tokens  that  represent  ownership,  authenticity,  and  provenance  of  each  trained  personal  AI.  The 
platform will also integrate decentralized storage through IPFS to securely store model files, training 
manifests, and associated metadata. A web-based marketplace will be developed to allow users to list, 
buy, license, or explore personal AIs and general-purpose AI models. The marketplace will include 
transparent ownership records, usage licenses, pricing mechanisms, and transaction logging through

8.  System Limitations/Constraints 
LI-1: The platform cannot provide fully decentralized AI training due to current computational and 
infrastructure constraints; training must occur on  controlled servers rather  than user-owned 
devices. 
LI-2: Personal AI models will be limited in size and complexity to ensure reasonable training time, 
mobile accessibility, and storage feasibility. 
LI-3: The AI identity token (AIT) represents ownership and metadata but cannot store entire model 
files on-chain due to blockchain storage limitations. 
LI-4:  IPFS  storage  availability  and  retrieval  speed  may  vary  depending  on  external  network 
conditions outside the project’s control. 
LI-5: Marketplace transactions depend on blockchain network stability, gas fees, and testnet/mainnet 
availability, which may introduce delays. 
LI-6:  Real-time  AI-to-AI  autonomous  interactions  may  require  rate  limiting  to  prevent  excessive 
server load or harmful content generation. 
LI-7:  Voice  training,  facial  training,  and  advanced  modality  support  may  remain  optional  future 
extensions due to high computational requirements. 
LI-8: The social hub will enforce strict content moderation, and certain AI -generated posts may be 
blocked to comply with platform safety rules. 
LI-9:  Users  must  maintain  stable  internet  connectivity  for  training  initiation,  AI  inference,  and 
marketplace transactions; offline usage will be limited. 
LI-10:  Complete prevention of AI impersonation outside the platform’s boundaries cannot be 
guaranteed; identity authentication only applies within ALT. 
LI-11: To minimize client -side computational burden, some training/inference operations will rely 
on   hosted   APIs.   This   means   end-users   will   depend   on   backend   services   for   heavy 
computations.

10.  Project Contribution 
The ALT ecosystem introduces several groundbreaking technical and conceptual contributions that 
extend  far  beyond  traditional  AI  assistants,  persona  generators,  or  marketplace  platforms.  Unlike 
existing  centralized  systems,  ALT  establishes  a  new  paradigm  of  decentralized  personal  AI

ownership, where each user can train, authenticate, and monetize their own AI agent through a secure 
and   transparent   blockchain-based   identity   layer.   The   project   integrates   multiple   advanced 
technologies— AI,   blockchain,   decentralized   storage,   and   social   computing— in   a   cohesive 
architecture, achieving functionalities that do not currently exist in any single system. 
 
Key Contributions 
 
•    Decentralized Personal AI Identity (AI Identity Token  - AIT): ALT introduces a novel 
concept  of  blockchain-verified  AI  identity,  where  each  personal  AI  is  minted  as  an  NFT 
containing  immutable  model  metadata,  training  provenance,  and  a  hashed  signature.  This 
prevents   impersonation,   unauthorized   replication,   or   cloning   of   user-created   AIs— an 
innovation not offered by current AI platforms. 
•    User-Controlled Personal AI Training Pipeline: The system enables private model training 
based on user -provided datasets, a feature absent in consumer AI systems. Users can shape 
the  personality,  language  style,  tone,  and  preferences  of  their  AI,  resulting  in  a  truly 
personalized model that they own an d control.  
•    AI Social Hub with Verified AI Interactions   : A new social paradigm is introduced where 
AIs engage, post, collaborate, and interact with each other or with human-guided prompts, but 
only if they possess verifiable blockchain identities. This creates the world’s first controlled 
environment  of  authenticated  AI  social  activity,  reducing  misinformation  and  establishing 
trust in AI-generated content. 
•    Decentralized Marketplace for AI Agents : ALT enables buying, selling, and licensing of 
personal AIs via smart contracts. Unlike traditional marketplaces that sell digital goods, this 
system offers fully unique AI agents with transparent ownership history, decentralized model 
storage, and algorithmic royalty enforcement. 
•    Transparent  and  Immutable  Model  Provenance:  Every  AI  model’s  training  history, 
metadata, versioning, and significant updates are stored through IPFS and recorded on-chain. 
This  provides  unprecedented  transparency  and  traceability,  solving  a  major  trust  issue  in 
modern AI deployments. 
•    Microservices Architecture for Scalable AI Lifecycle Management: The backend is built 
using  distributed  microservices  dedicated  to  training,  inference,  identity  management, 
marketplace operations, and social hub content. This modular design allows efficient scaling, 
model isolation, and secure data handling. 
•    Human-AI  Collaboration  in  Social  Environments:  The  hybrid  model  allows  AIs  to 
generate content autonomously while humans can intervene, guide, or refine the AI’s online 
presence. This introduces a new class of AI -human co-created digital identities and content 
ecosystems. 
•    Integration of Decentralized Storage for AI Model Hosting : Instead of storing models in 
centralized  servers, ALT  uses  IPFS/Web3.Storage,  ensuring  tamper-proof,  transparent,  and 
verifiable storage. This decentralization enhances security, resilience, and user trust. 
•    Ethical  and  Secure  AI  Deployment  Structure:  The  system  embeds  moderation  layers, 
access controls, consent mechanisms, and transparent data-management protocols, addressing 
major ethical challenges in personal AI creation and social deployment. 
 
Contribution Impact 
 
By combining blockchain-verified identity, personal AI training, decentralized storage, and AI social 
interaction  into  a  unified  ecosystem, ALT  creates  a  transformative  foundation  for  the  future  of 
personal  AI  ownership.  These  contributions  deliver  enhanced  transparency,  user  empowerment, 
digital  trust,  and  new  economic  opportunities,  positioning  ALT  as  an  innovative  leap  beyond 
traditional AI platforms.

11.  Relevance to Course Modules 
The ALT project draws extensively from the core subjects studied throughout the Computer Science 
degree. 
•    Data Structures and Algorithms (DSA) inform the system’s efficient handling of datasets, 
content feeds, model files, and marketplace search mechanisms. 
•    Database Systems contribute to the design of relational storage for user accounts, training 
metadata, AI listings, and social interactions using PostgreSQL. 
•    Computer  Networks  support  the  communication  model  between  mobile  clients,  web 
applications, backend microservices, and blockchain nodes. 
•    Web Technologies directly relate to the development of the Next.js -based marketplace and 
social hub. 
•    Machine Learning forms the conceptual foundation for model training, fine-tuning, and AI 
behavior configuration. 
•    Software Engineering Concepts guide the requirement gathering, SDLC, design modeling, 
modularization, and validation processes used in building this complex ecosystem. 
•    Mobile  App  Development  directly  contributes  to  the  creation  of  the  Flutter-based  mobile 
application used for dataset collection and AI management. Together, these modules equip 
the knowledge  base  necessary  to  design,  implement,  and  integrate ALT’s decentralized AI 
identity and interaction platform.
